## Version A — Short post (~170 words)

> On 2 August the EU AI Act's main obligations became applicable — including the deployer duties in Article 26.
>
> Every compliance whitepaper in my inbox this month is written for model providers: GPAI documentation, training data summaries, conformity assessments. Useful. Aimed at someone else.
>
> If your company *runs* AI agents rather than trains models, you're a deployer. Your list is shorter and it starts now:
>
> 1. **Operate per the provider's instructions** — and be able to show each agent's configuration history.
> 2. **Assign human oversight** — with a record that intervention happened when it should have.
> 3. **Retain the automatically generated logs for at least six months** (Art. 26(6)).
>
> That last one has a quiet implication: the logs you must produce next February are the ones your agents are generating right now. Record-keeping is the one obligation you cannot backfill.
>
> And a mutable log in a vendor dashboard isn't a record. If you can't verify it offline, it's not evidence.
>
> We keep a deployer-side checklist current here: https://www.ai-identity.co/eu-ai-act-checklist
>
> #EUAIAct #AIAgents #AIGovernance #AICompliance
